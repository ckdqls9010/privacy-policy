Frequently Asked Questions

How do I restore my Remove Ads purchase?

Open the app → Menu → tap "Remove Ads". The app will automatically detect your previous purchase. If you still see ads, please ensure you are signed in with the same Apple ID / Google account used at purchase time. Contact us if the issue persists.

I lost my game progress.

Game progress is stored locally on your device. Reinstalling or wiping the app will reset progress. We do not currently sync progress across devices.

How do I change the language?

Menu → Language. Eight languages supported: Korean, English, Japanese, Chinese (Simplified/Traditional), Spanish, French, Portuguese.

The app crashes / is laggy.

Please try: (1) close other apps, (2) restart your device, (3) update to the latest version of the app from the App Store / Play Store. If the issue persists, email us with your device model and OS version.
